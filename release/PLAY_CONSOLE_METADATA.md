## Full Description

English:

Jew in Time: every mitzvah in its time. The app calculates halachic times on your device from your location and reminds you inside each mitzvah's exact window.

Mitzvot, on time
- Nine time-bound mitzvot: tefillin, Shema, Shacharit, Mincha, Maariv, morning blessings, candle lighting, Havdalah and the Omer
- Add your own mitzvot, with reminders you set
- Two buttons on the notification itself: "Done" and "Open text"
- A home screen sorted by urgency: what is open now, what comes next, what has passed
- Day, week and month schedule, and a history with a day streak
- Hebrew calendar, parasha, holidays and halachic opinions, set for Israel or the Diaspora

A siddur in four nusachim
The prayer text for each mitzvah, plus 13 more blessings and prayers: Grace After Meals, Me'ein Shalosh, Borei Nefashot, the Traveler's Prayer, Asher Yatzar, blessings on food and fragrance, blessings on sights and sounds, the bedtime Shema, Kiddush Levana, affixing a mezuzah, Sheva Berachot, Chanukah candle lighting and Tikkun HaKlali. Ashkenaz, Sefard, Edot HaMizrach or Chabad, adjusted to the day, with auto-scroll so you can read without touching the screen.

Shabbat and Yom Tov
The app rests on Shabbat and Yom Tov: no notifications and a quiet screen. When it ends, mark what you did so your streak holds. An hour before it begins, a notice gives candle-lighting and exit times.

Hilulot of tzaddikim
Optional notices for the hilulot of 57 tzaddikim: the evening before and the evening it begins.

Rosh Chodesh
Optional notices for every Rosh Chodesh: three days before, the evening before and on the day itself.

Weekly report
After Shabbat, a notification opens your report for the week: what you marked, what to improve and what the coming week holds. Past reports are in the history tab. On by default, and you can switch it off in Settings.

Family purity tracking (optional)
Track the count, the bedikot and the mikveh night, with reminders by halachic times and the minhag you choose. Offered to married users only and off until you turn it on. Notification wording is discreet, the screens are locked with biometrics, and the data is kept encrypted on your device only. The app does not rule on halacha; in any doubt, ask a rav.

Private by design
- No account and no server: your settings, history and data stay on your device
- 20 cities to choose from, or GPS
- Hebrew and English, and six themes: gold, pink, purple, blue, dark and plum
- The app sends only technical crash reports, with no personal information

## Data Safety Answers

Declare exactly this. The source of truth is the published policy in [../docs/privacy/index.html](../docs/privacy/index.html) and [../docs/privacy/en/index.html](../docs/privacy/en/index.html).

- Location, approximate and precise: optional (the user can pick a city instead), used for app functionality (zmanim). Processed on the device and never transmitted. Not shared.
- Crash logs and diagnostics: collected, in production builds only, and shared with Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), the crash-reporting processor. Content: stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and update id. Not linked to identity: no personal data, no taharah data, no screen content, no IP address stored. Purpose: app functionality. Collection is required, since the app has no in-app switch for it. Data is encrypted in transit.
- Health info: the app stores menstrual-cycle related dates on the device only and does not collect them. Do not declare Health info as collected or shared.
- Not collected: name and phone (optional profile fields, shown in Settings and kept on the device), gender and marital status (profile answers, kept on the device), contacts, messages, photos, files, audio, calendar, financial info, web history, device or other IDs, purchase history.
- Deletion: the app has no accounts. Settings, Reset the app, erases everything stored on the device; uninstalling does the same. Crash reports are not linked to a person, so they cannot be matched to a deletion request.
- Security practices: data in transit is encrypted; no data is sold; no ads SDK.

Also check Play Console, App content, for the Health apps declaration: the taharah tracker may trigger it. Answer it with the same facts: device-only, nothing collected, no medical advice or diagnosis.

Default current app behavior:

- Core mitzvah data remains on device
- No account required
- No data selling
