# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own pure helper logic for timeline, history, weekly report, liturgical-day, custom-mitzvah, Chok LeYisrael portion and reader text, and taharat hamishpacha (niddah cycle) calculations.

## Ownership

- [buildDayTimeline.ts](buildDayTimeline.ts) owns day schedule item construction (`urgent` and `expired` only on the app day) and `currentOrNextWindow()`, which checks yesterday too, because night windows (omer, maariv) stay open past midnight, and looks up to nine days ahead past skipped windows (Sukkot in Israel keeps tefillin off for a week). It steps the location's calendar days from the app day (`appDay()`), each the device-local midnight of that date with zmanim at the location's noon, so the date it returns keys the same day as that window's notification. `dayWindow(mitzvah, date, location, settings)` is the one way to read a mitzvah's window on a day (`null` when it has none or `isSkippedAt()` skips it) and returns a `DayWindow` (`start`, `end`, `date`); `appDayWindow()` is the window home shows: one open now, whatever day it was computed for (maariv after midnight, a window still open past alot), else the one whose start falls in this app day (the day's own, or the next date's when it starts before alot, such as a custom window at 00:30), else none.
- [historyStats.ts](historyStats.ts) owns streak, daily, per-mitzvah, and missed-yesterday statistics. `judgeDay()` sorts every item of a day into done, missed or still open: a skip settles an item at once, and a mitzvah switched on after the day ended is not judged on it. A day ends at `appDayEnd()`, the next alot, and outside a block an item stays open until both its day and its window have ended, so a window that runs past midnight (maariv, the Omer, Chok LeYisrael until the next alot) is not missed while it can still be marked. Today's row, `missedYesterday` and the streak's walk start at `appDay(now, location)`, so between midnight and alot today is still the evening's date. `computeStreak()` walks back from today with no cap, through the archive past retention, to the oldest recorded day, and with nothing enabled stops at the first unmarked day. Each `perMitzvah` entry counts `done`, `eligible` (decided: done plus missed) and `pending` (still open), and `isTrackedInRange(stat)` (`eligible > 0 || pending > 0`) is the one rule for a mitzvah that was relevant on at least one day of the range; the history tab lists only those, so a mitzvah out of season (Sefirat HaOmer in Tishrei) never shows.
- [streakGoal.ts](streakGoal.ts) owns the history ring's goals: `STREAK_GOALS` (7, 30, 100, 365), `nextStreakGoal(streak)` (the first goal above the streak, then the next multiple of 365) and `streakGoalProgress(streak)` (the streak as a share of the next goal, the owner's call on 2026-10-21; 0 only at 0 days); a negative, fractional or non-finite streak counts as its whole days, never below 0.
- [checkIn.ts](checkIn.ts) owns the post-block check-in: which mitzvot it asks about (every window that reaches into the block, erev included, and one that ends exactly at the block's start counts as reaching into it, as Chok LeYisrael does on a Friday or an erev; but not one skipped or switched on after the block), `blockForDay()` (a day's own block or the one it is the erev of), its deadline (`checkInDeadline(block, location)`: the alot that ends the first weekday after the block, `appDayEnd()` of that day, 04:00 on the location's clock where there are no zmanim) and `checkInPending(block, checkIns, now, location)`, which tests it, `latestCheckIn()`, and the pending ids the screens show as waiting.
- [weeklyReport.ts](weeklyReport.ts) owns the weekly report, pure over `HistoryInput`: `weekEndFor(block)` (a block that holds a Saturday closes a week, and its last day is the week end, so a Shabbat followed by Yom Tov ends on the Yom Tov's last day; `null` for any other block), `shabbatOf()`, `weekStartFor()` (a week is the days after the previous week end up to and including its own, so a Yom Tov that extends a week past Shabbat belongs to one week only: Rosh Hashana 5787 gives an eight-day week and then a six-day one; seven days when there is no previous week end), `weekSummary(input, weekStart, weekEnd, now)` (each day judged by `judgeDay()` so a Shabbat item still waiting in an open check-in is pending, not missed; per-day and per-mitzvah done / missed / pending counts, where `pending` holds every item still open, tonight's windows after the block's exit included, and `checkInPending` only the unmarked items of the open check-in of that week's Shabbat block, 0 once it is finished or past its deadline, which is what the report's `/checkin` banner counts; the best day, `computeStreak()` as of `now`, and the mitzvah with the most misses as `improve`), `upcomingWeek()` (the days after the week end up to and including the next week end, the next Saturday extended by its block, seven days when that block is unknown; each day that holds something: `getHolidays()` with chol hamoed, Hoshana Raba and erev days switched on, plus, while the hilulot switch is on, one `report.hilula` line for a day that has hilulot, their names joined by ', ') and the week navigation `weekEndOnOrBefore()`, `previousWeekEnd()` and `nextWeekEnd()`, which only count a week whose block has ended at `now`; `reportWeekEnd()` picks the week the screen opens on, anchored on `appDay(now, location)`. `calendarLocale()` picks the vowel-free Hebrew labels. The screen is [../app/report.tsx](../app/report.tsx), the notice [../services/notifications/planners/report.ts](../services/notifications/planners/report.ts).
- [chok.ts](chok.ts) owns which Chok LeYisrael portion applies: `ChokSlot` (`sun` to `thu`, `thuNight` for ליל שישי, `fri`) with `CHOK_SLOTS` in that order, and `chokSlotsOf()` (`VEZOT_HABERACHA` has no `fri`). `chokParashiyot(shabbat, inIsrael)` follows the rule of hebcal's `findParshaHaShavua()` (`@hebcal/leyning`), written here, not copied (the user's choice, 2026-10-11): the parasha read on that Shabbat by `inIsrael`, both names in a double week, Vezot Haberakhah for a festival Shabbat on 3–22 Tishrei in Israel or 3–23 abroad, else the next parasha read on a Shabbat that is not a festival. A week is the days up to its Shabbat (`onOrAfter(6)`). `chokPortionAt(instant, location, inIsrael)` is the reader's default: `null` strictly inside a holy block; the learning day runs from alot to the next alot; Thursday from its nightfall (tzeit, or the exit when Thursday closes a block; civil midnight without zmanim) until Friday's alot is `thuNight`; after a block's exit the next day's learning has begun, so motzaei Shabbat is `sun` of the next week and the night after a Yom Tov on Sunday to Wednesday is the next weekday. `chokPortionOn(day, location, inIsrael)` takes a device calendar day: Sunday to Friday by weekday, never `thuNight`, `null` on Shabbat, Yom Tov and the Friday of a Vezot Haberakhah week. `chokMakeUpsBefore(day, location, inIsrael)` lists the earlier days of the same week from Sunday, keyed by `dateKey()`, each with its weekday slot and `holy` for a Shabbat or Yom Tov day, which stays in the list; `chokOpenMakeUps()` leaves out what is marked. The parasha follows the `inIsrael` argument (the user's setting); holy days follow `location.inIsrael` through `HebcalService`, as on every other surface. `ChokSlot` lives in [../types/chok.ts](../types/chok.ts) and is re-exported here. The file also builds the reader's text: `CHOK_SECTION_KINDS`; `HAFTARAH_VARIANT`, the one place the haftarah follows the nusach (Edot HaMizrach the Sephardi one, every other nusach the Ashkenazi one); `chokParashaName()` and `chokWeekName()` (the Hebrew name from hebcal's `he-x-NoNikud` locale, the English one as hebcal spells it, a double week joined by a maqaf or a hyphen); `chokReaderPortion({ now, date, slot }, location, inIsrael)`, the date's portion or else this moment's, and when there is none the latest slot of that week before the day (the Friday of Vezot Haberakhah opens Thursday night, a Shabbat date Friday), with a requested slot replacing the slot when the week has it; `chokSlotSections(asset, slot, variant)` (a slot's sections with that variant's haftarah); and `chokText(assets, { slot, choice, variant, kindNames, variantNames })`, the `SiddurText` the reader resolves: one parasha titled by its printed headings with English kind names, except that a haftarah section that carries a `variant` is titled in English by that variant's name (`variantNames`, "Haftarah (Sephardi)" or "Haftarah (Ashkenazi)", translated by the reader like `kindNames`), so only a haftarah the source prints in one version keeps the plain "Haftarah"; Hebrew titles stay the printed headings; or in a double week the first, the second or both one after the other, each title then prefixed with the parasha's Hebrew name and the first section of each parasha opening a chapter, so titles stay unique and the picker lists the two parashiyot. The reader's tracking of the Chok LeYisrael mitzvah is pure here too: `chokSlotDay(portion)` is the device calendar day of a portion's slot (Thursday night is Thursday); `chokToday(now, location, inIsrael)` is the learning day in effect, the day of `chokPortionAt()`, or the location's civil day when it is `null` (the Friday of Vezot Haberakhah); `chokDoneDay(portion, today)` is the day the reader's "סיימתי" marks, a day slot of today's week up to today, never `thuNight`, else `null`; `chokOpenMakeUps(today, location, inIsrael, completions, enabledAt)` is `chokMakeUpsBefore()` without the days marked done for `chok_leyisrael` and the days that ended before `enabledAt` (a day ends at `appDayEnd()`, the rule of `wasEnabledBy()`), Yom Tov days kept; and `chokDayHasText(day, inIsrael)` is true when content exists, the Hebrew day has a weekday portion and every parasha of its week has an asset (Shabbat and Yom Tov are the caller's question, `hasSiddurText()` asks `dayFeatures()`). `chokText()` takes `makeUps` (`ChokMakeUpFold`: date key, slot and label) and puts one section per make-up day before the slot's sections: titled and folded (`optional`) by its label, holding that day's sections of the same assets, choice and variant, each opened by a bold heading segment (the printed heading, the English title as `en`), with no chapter.
- [skipRules.ts](skipRules.ts) owns the single `skipOn` predicate shared by the scheduler, timeline, home, and history, including `keepsCholHamoed()` (tefillin on chol hamoed only for nusach Ashkenaz abroad), and the quiet window: `quietBlockAt()` / `isQuietAt()` (strictly inside a holy block), `opensQuietBlock()` for a trigger on a block's opening edge, `nextQuietBoundary()` for live switching, `reminderFires()` (ahead, inside its window, not quiet) shared by the scheduler and the next-reminder preview, and `holyBlockLabelKeys()` naming a block for the notice and the Shabbat screen.
- [siddur.ts](siddur.ts) owns the liturgical day of a text (`liturgicalDay()`), the day flags derived from Hebcal (`dayFeatures()`), condition matching, and resolving a text for a day. The "yesterday" flags (`roshChodeshYesterday`, `cholHamoedPesachYesterday`, `cholHamoedSukkotYesterday`, `chanukahYesterday`, `purimYesterday`) name what the previous Hebrew day was, for a meal begun before sunset, and hold only when the day carries no insert of that kind itself. `chanukahFirstNight` is 25 Kislev, `kiddushLevana` the 3rd to the 15th of any month (the widest window; the text's notes give the customs), and `avBeforeTishaBav` Av up to the observed fast, the 10th when the 9th is Shabbat. The selichot flags (`selichotFlags()`) hold on no Shabbat, Yom Tov or Yom Kippur: `selichotSephardi` from 2 Elul (`SEPHARDI_SELICHOT_FROM_ELUL`) through 9 Tishrei; `selichotAshkenazi` from Leil Selichot, the Sunday that leaves at least four selichot days before Rosh Hashana (`leilSelichot()`), through 9 Tishrei; and the day the Ashkenazi books print a selichot for: `selichotDay1` to `selichotDay7` (the Nth weekday from Leil Selichot), `erevRoshHashana`, `tzomGedaliah`, `selichotTeshuva2` to `selichotTeshuva5` (the Nth weekday from 3 Tishrei, Tzom Gedaliah the first) and `erevYomKippur`, the order of Wikisource's "טבלת סליחות אשכנז" without its footnoted swap of the fourth and fifth Ten Days selichot. Resolution trims the whitespace a filtered-out alternative leaves at a paragraph's edges, and drops an optional or minyan label whose own `when` does not hold that day. `segmentBlocks(segments, field, offset)` groups consecutive segments that share one kind of label into one block. The reader groups a section by `optional` first, then each block by `minyan`; `offset` keeps the inner blocks' start indices in section terms. `forMaritalStatus(sections, status)` is applied to the resolved sections before anything else draws them: for a `single` reader it drops every section whose own `optional` label, and every segment whose `optional` label, carries `marriedOnly` (Chabad's bedtime passage before marital relations), and a section left with no segment; for `married` and `null` it returns the sections untouched, so the passage keeps its fold. `aloneFolds(section, mode)` returns that grouping for a prayer mode, the one tree the reader draws from: praying alone, a `minyanOnly` section and every labeled minyan block are `folded`, and an optional block (or section) whose label has `alone: true` is `openByDefault`; with a minyan nothing is folded or open by default. `siddurLineHeight()` is the Hebrew line height the reader draws, and `autoScrollPixelsPerSecond()` turns an auto-scroll pace in lines per minute into pixels at a text size. `pickerEntries(sections)` is what the reader's section picker lists for a resolved text: its chapters (each at the section whose `chapter` opens it), or every section when the text has none; `pickerEntryAt(entries, sections, index)` names the entry a section belongs to, the last chapter at or before it, or the section itself before the first chapter.
- [locationDay.ts](locationDay.ts) owns `locationNoon()`, which maps a device calendar day to the same date at the location, and the app's day (the user's call, 2026-10-12: the night belongs to the evening before): `appDay(instant, location)` is the location's date, except that from local midnight until alot hashachar it is still the previous date, as the device-local midnight of that date; `appDayKey()` is its `dateKey()`, and `appDayEnd(day, location)` is the instant that day ends, the next date's alot. The boundary itself is `HebcalService.dawnAt()` (04:00 on the location's clock where the sun neither rises nor sets), its one home. Every surface that shows or files "today" (home, history and the streak, the weekly report, the schedule, the day view, the reader's default date, the check-in deadline) reads `appDay()` or `appDayEnd()`; never `dateKey(new Date())`.
- [clock.ts](clock.ts) owns `TIME_PATTERN`, the `HH:mm` check every typed time passes, `timeToMinutes()`, which orders two valid times, and its inverse `minutesToTime()` (minutes into a day as `HH:mm`, shared by `reminderClock()`, the reminder editor and the mitzvah screen's clock reminders). The custom mitzvah form and the taharah log both import the first two. It also owns the two display formats every screen shares: `formatRemaining(ms, t)`, a time left as minutes (`time.unit.minutes`) under an hour and `H:MM` with `time.unit.hours` from an hour up, never negative; and `clockOf(date, zone?)`, an instant as `HH:mm` on the device clock, or on the clock of `zone` when given. `clockRange(start, end, t)` is a window as two such clocks, `detail.timeRange`, or `detail.timeRangeNextDay` when the end falls on a later device calendar day than the start (Chok LeYisrael until the next alot, the Omer, a summer maariv); the mitzvah screen and the day drilldown print windows through it.
- [customMitzvah.ts](customMitzvah.ts) owns the pure rules of a custom mitzvah, shared by its form and [../data/customMitzvotAdapter.ts](../data/customMitzvotAdapter.ts): `WEEKDAYS`; `windowMinutes(start, end)`, the length of a window: `end - start` when positive, else that plus 1440, so an end before the start runs into the next day and equal times are a full 24 hours; `categoryForWindow()`, where a window of `ALL_DAY_MIN` (480) minutes or more is `daily-allday` and any other is sorted by its midpoint, `(start + length / 2) % 1440` (before `NIGHT_END_MIN`, 04:00, `daily-evening`, a night window; then before 12:00 `daily-morning`, before 17:00 `daily-afternoon`, else `daily-evening`); `isActiveOn()` (no `activeDays` means every day, an empty list none); and `reminderClock()`, the `HH:mm` a reminder fires at inside the window (an `end` anchor counts from `start + windowMinutes()`, so a window across midnight prints the clock of the next day), `null` when either time fails `TIME_PATTERN` or the trigger falls before the start or past the window's length. It also owns the form's content-block rules: `DraftBlock` (a `ContentBlock`, or a siddur block whose `textId` is still `null` while the user has not chosen), `cleanContentBlock()` (trims the Hebrew; drops a block with no text, a link with no url, a siddur block with no chosen text or whose `textId` fails `isSiddurTextId()`, so a retired id such as `tzitzit` is dropped on the next save), `cleanContentBlocks()` (the complete blocks in order, `undefined` when none remain) and `siddurTextName(id, language)` (a standalone or mitzvah text's name, `null` for an id the registries no longer know, in which case the form shows the name stored on the block).
- [mitzvahName.ts](mitzvahName.ts) owns `mitzvahName(mitzvah, language)`, the one choice of a mitzvah's displayed name: the English name in English when it has one, else the Hebrew name. [../hooks/useDayModel.ts](../hooks/useDayModel.ts) exposes it as `nameFor()`.
- [taharah/](taharah/) owns the niddah-cycle engine, pure functions over raw events, `TaharahRules` and a `Location`. Its shapes live in [../types/taharah.ts](../types/taharah.ts) and its rule presets in [../data/taharahPresets.ts](../data/taharahPresets.ts):
  - [taharah/onot.ts](taharah/onot.ts) owns day and onah arithmetic. A Hebrew day is its hebcal absolute number (`abs`), and its night onah comes before its day onah, so `onahIndex()` is `abs * 2` plus 1 for a day onah and onot are counted with integers. `onahAt()` resolves an instant in the location's zone: between shkia and tzeit it names the ending day onah and flags it doubtful. `onsetCandidates()` gives both onot a doubtful onset may belong to. `onahBounds()` runs a night onah from the previous shkia to sunrise and a day onah from sunrise to shkia. `tevilaBlockedOnNight()` and `nextTevilaNight()` skip the night of Yom Kippur and of the Tisha B'Av fast, and only those: hebcal flags "Erev Tish'a B'Av" as a major fast too, so the `EREV` flag is excluded, or 8 Av and a deferred-fast Shabbat 9 Av would block a tevila.
  - [taharah/cycle.ts](taharah/cycle.ts) owns the replay of the raw events, ordered by `eventDay()` then `recordedAt`: `deriveCycle()` gives the current `CycleState`, `replayCycles()` one `CycleRecord` per onset since the last pause (the settled hefsek and tevila of each), and `hefsekOutcome()` names why a hefsek would not count (`noCycle`, `tahor`, `notClean`, `tooEarly`, `restartsCount`) from the very rule the replay applies, for the log screen's guard. A doubtful onset is counted from the next day. A hefsek that is not clean, comes before `hefsekEarliestDay`, follows a tevila or lands inside a running count is ignored. Bleeding on a bedika restarts the count with a new hefsek and no wait. A doubtful bedika, or no bedika on clean day 1 or 7, holds the cycle in `safek` until a ruling; a ruling to continue settles only the clean days before the day it was given. A pause drops the cycle, so whatever follows starts with a new onset. A tevila closes the cycle even without a hefsek (the husband records none). A first tevila night on a blocked night is deferred to the next allowed one (`earliestTevilaNight()`). For the husband, `tevilaEstimatedDay` is the night after seven clean days from the earliest hefsek day, and `husbandTevilaNight()` / `husbandTevilaOn()` offer it for `HUSBAND_TEVILA_WINDOW_NIGHTS` nights unless he records the tevila.
  - [taharah/vestot.ts](taharah/vestot.ts) owns the onot a woman separates in: `perishaOnot()`, `yomHachodeshOf()`, `haflagaDays()`, `activeOnsets()` (everything after the last pause; a resume clears nothing, as in `deriveCycle()`) and `kavuaHints()`. Only the last haflaga interval is kept, and a confirmed veset kavua is only hinted, never from a run that includes a doubtful sighting. A doubtful last onset yields both candidates for every reason, and a doubtful previous onset both intervals, so the shorter one is never lost. Under `haflagaMethod: 'onot'` (Chabad) the haflaga is counted in onot from the night after the previous hefsek and laid from the night after the new one, both read from `replayCycles()` so a hefsek the cycle rejected anchors nothing: the day count stands in without a previous hefsek, and without the new hefsek it is not yet known. A yom hachodesh on the 30th of a month followed by a 29-day month moves to the day after its 29th and is flagged `disputed`. Where the rules keep ohr zarua, it precedes each onah except one that is only the onah beinonit of day 31.
  - [taharah/tasks.ts](taharah/tasks.ts) owns `taharahTasksFor()` and `taskKindsForRole()`. A task list is keyed by a device calendar day, the day `dateKey()` names: the daytime of its Hebrew day, plus the night onah of the next Hebrew day, which opens at that day's shkia and so lands on the evening before its own date. The tevila task starts at `HebcalService.nightfall()`, the block's exit on motzaei Shabbat / Yom Tov, else tzeit. It is projected from the cycle state at `now`, so a hefsek not yet done keeps appearing on every later eligible day. An onah kept only as ohr zarua gets a `perisha` task but no `bedikaVeset`. The husband sees only `perisha`, `tevila` and `expectOnset`; his perisha onot are not gated on the tahor stage, and his `tevila` task comes from the estimated night.
  - [taharah/summary.ts](taharah/summary.ts) owns the one stage line home and the dashboard share. `visibleStage()` folds the husband's niddah-side stages (awaiting hefsek, shiva nekiim, safek) into niddah, except that with the current onah and location it shows his estimated mikveh night as `tevilaNight` / `awaitingTevila` (hint `taharah.stage.tevilaEstimated.hint`). `stageHint()` returns a `Hint` (an i18n key with params; a param that is itself a key goes in `translate`) and takes the caller's `HintFormat` for civil dates and clock times, so home keeps its short date and the dashboard its long one. `renderHint()` turns a `Hint` into text. `nextPerishaOnah()` finds the next perisha onah from now, and `onahStartDate()` gives the civil day it is shown on, a night onah on the evening before.
- [settingsSearch.ts](settingsSearch.ts) owns the settings search: `SETTINGS_SEARCH_ITEMS` (id, icon, title and label keys, synonyms in both languages, the city names, and a `section` or a `route`, with an optional `when` condition), `buildSettingsSearchIndex(lookup)` (each item's words from both dictionaries, which the caller supplies, so the module imports no store), `normalizeSearchText()` (no nikud or accents, no quotes or geresh, final letters as regular ones, lower case), `typedInOtherLayout()` (the query re-read on the other keyboard, Hebrew for Latin keys and back) and `searchSettings(query, index, context)`: every word of the query must match, exact over prefix over substring, a title word scoring higher, up to two Hebrew prefix letters stripped at a small cost; typo tolerance (a swap of two letters for a four-letter word, one edit from five letters, two from eight) runs only when the strict pass finds nothing; at most `SETTINGS_SEARCH_LIMIT` (8) results.
- Utility tests live in [__tests__/](__tests__/).

## Local Contracts

- Keep utilities pure: no direct Zustand, MMKV, navigation, notifications, or native side effects.
- Accept required services/data as inputs instead of importing route state.
- Preserve local-date and halachic-location assumptions from [../services/AGENTS.md](../services/AGENTS.md) and [../data/AGENTS.md](../data/AGENTS.md).
- [buildDayTimeline.ts](buildDayTimeline.ts) and [historyStats.ts](historyStats.ts) take location dates held as device-local midnights, the day `dateKey()` names. They resolve zmanim and the day's observance from `locationNoon(day, location)`, never from the day's device-local midnight, which is the previous day at a location west of the device.
- Zmanim can be unavailable: [buildDayTimeline.ts](buildDayTimeline.ts) returns an empty timeline and [historyStats.ts](historyStats.ts) records a zero-eligibility day when `getZmanim()` returns `null`.
- Never re-implement the `skipOn` check. Any surface that decides whether a mitzvah applies must call `isSkippedAt()` from [skipRules.ts](skipRules.ts), or the surfaces disagree with each other.
- An item is missed only once it can no longer be marked: the end of its day (the next alot, `appDayEnd()`) or of its window, whichever is later, or the close of its block's check-in (the alot that ends the first weekday after the block). Percentages count only decided items, and the streak treats an open day as neither kept nor broken. Shabbat and Yom Tov have no special case beyond that.
- Never re-implement the quiet window either. The scheduler and the Shabbat screen both read `quietBlockAt()`; its edges stay open so the candle-lighting reminder and havdalah fire.
- [chok.ts](chok.ts) decides nothing about the calendar itself: holy days come from `HebcalService.isHolyDay()`, the quiet block from `quietBlockAt()`, the learning day's alot from `HebcalService.hebrewNightAt()` (its 04:00 fallback included), Thursday's nightfall from `HebcalService.nightfall()`, and the location day from `locationNoon()` and `civilHebrewDayAt()`. A change to any of those rules goes there, never into a copy in [chok.ts](chok.ts).
- Chok LeYisrael product decisions, confirmed by the user on 2026-10-12. Each lives in one named place in [chok.ts](chok.ts), and the assets carry both haftarah variants and every slot, so flipping one is a code change plus a dated test in [__tests__/chok.test.ts](__tests__/chok.test.ts) or [__tests__/chokText.test.ts](__tests__/chokText.test.ts), never a rebuild:
  - Israel or abroad follows the user's `inIsrael` setting (`chokParashiyot(shabbat, inIsrael)`), because the weekly parasha splits between Israel and abroad when a second day of Yom Tov falls on Shabbat, and every other surface of the app already reads the reading from the same setting. To follow the location instead, pass `location.inIsrael` at the callers.
  - The haftarah is the Sephardi one for Edot HaMizrach and the Ashkenazi one for Ashkenaz, Sefard and Chabad (`HAFTARAH_VARIANT`), because the source prints exactly those two variants and has no Sefard or Chabad variant of its own, so the nuschaot of Ashkenazi origin take the Ashkenazi one. To let the reader choose, replace the map lookup with a persisted user field.
  - Thursday night (`thuNight`, ליל שישי) is readable but never tracked on its own: `chokDoneDay()` returns `null` for it, so it has no "סיימתי", no make-up fold and no streak day, because the mitzvah is one portion a day and the Thursday-night reading is an addition the source prints beside Thursday's. To track it, give `thuNight` a done day in `chokDoneDay()` and a window in `CHOK_LEYISRAEL`.
  - The Friday of a Vezot Haberakhah week has no portion (`VEZOT_SLOTS` drops `fri`), because the source prints none: the week ends with the Torah reading of Simchat Torah. The build enforces the same shape (`validate.js`, Vezot Haberakhah without Friday).
- Every decision about which liturgical insert applies on a day goes through `dayFeatures()` and `matchesCondition()` in [siddur.ts](siddur.ts). Never add a second copy of a day rule elsewhere.
- The [taharah/](taharah/) engine stores nothing: events are the only input and every state, perisha onah and task is derived by replay, so a corrected rule is a replay, not a migration. It never reads a device-local getter for a halachic question: days are `abs` numbers and instants resolve in the location's zone.
- Judge the skip at the mitzvah window's own `start` instant, after `computeWindow()`, never at "now" and never at civil midnight. `HebcalService.isShabbat` is instant-sensitive, so any other instant makes the answer depend on when the caller happened to ask.
