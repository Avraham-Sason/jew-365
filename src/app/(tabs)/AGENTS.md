# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own the main bottom-tab experience: home, schedule, history, siddur, settings, and tab layout.

## Ownership

- [_layout.tsx](_layout.tsx) owns tab registration and tab bar behavior.
- [home.tsx](home.tsx), [schedule.tsx](schedule.tsx), [history.tsx](history.tsx), [siddur.tsx](siddur.tsx), and [settings.tsx](settings.tsx) own their respective tab screens.
- [index.tsx](index.tsx) redirects hidden tab index traffic.

## Local Contracts

- Tab labels and normal UI copy must stay in [../../i18n/AGENTS.md](../../i18n/AGENTS.md).
- Every tab screen opens with [NavBar](../../components/NavBar.tsx), whose `actions` slot holds the screen's header controls (home's counter). Status strips are [Banner](../../components/Banner.tsx), section headings [SectionLabel](../../components/SectionLabel.tsx), pickers and confirmations [BottomSheet](../../components/BottomSheet.tsx) and [ConfirmDialog](../../components/ConfirmDialog.tsx), and a mitzvah's icon comes from `iconFor(mitzvah.icon)`: a tab never keeps a local copy of any of them, nor a text glyph in place of the icon tile.
- Schedule's day / week / month toggle is a header-tone [SegmentedControl](../../components/SegmentedControl.tsx) in the dark band under the NavBar, and its previous / next arrows are [HeaderPill](../../components/ScreenHeader.tsx)s.
- The tab order in [_layout.tsx](_layout.tsx) is schedule, history, home, siddur, settings: home stays the initial route and sits in the middle of the bar, where [BottomTabs](../../components/BottomTabs.tsx) draws it as a raised gold disc (the user's choice, 2026-10-08). Keep it third when adding a tab.
- Schedule and history views should reuse pure helpers from [../../utils/AGENTS.md](../../utils/AGENTS.md) instead of duplicating day computation.
- Day navigation should route to [../day/AGENTS.md](../day/AGENTS.md) for drilldown behavior.
- Home shows a banner into `/checkin` while a check-in is open, and keeps the mitzvot waiting in it out of "missed". The schedule's day view labels them waiting.
- Home's header carries the date once: the NavBar title and subtitle. A missed card shows `home.passedAt` with the window's end and no ribbon, never the "expiring soon" state of a live card. The "completed" card lists skipped mitzvot too, labelled `state.skipped`, with the same undo as a completion, so a skip is visible and reversible. The long-press sheet is a [BottomSheet](../../components/BottomSheet.tsx) with two `SheetAction`s, details and skip; "edit" opened the same screen as details and is gone. Banner tones map to meaning: `warning` for missing zmanim, location or GPS and for the last-error banner, `accent` for the check-in and the profile prompt, `urgent` for denied notifications, `safe` for a ready update. `Banner` picks the text colour apart from the border colour, because the status colours read under 3:1 on their tinted backgrounds.
- Home calls `downloadNewUpdate()` on every return to the foreground and shows a reload banner while `useUpdates().isUpdatePending` holds. Settings shows `appVersionLabel()`. Both come from [../../services/appUpdates.ts](../../services/appUpdates.ts). Home's foreground permission sync and scheduling refresh, and Settings' permission sync on focus, report a failure through `reportError('schedule', …)` instead of swallowing it, which is what raises the last-error banner.
- Settings is a column of four [SettingsSection](../../components/SettingsSection.tsx)s in this order: `settings.profile`, `settings.prayer`, `settings.mitzvot`, `settings.device`. Profile holds the fields (name, phone, gender, marital status; the status labels follow the gender) and the taharah block. Prayer holds the nusach chips, the `settings.prayerMode` chips (`prayerMode.minyan` or `prayerMode.alone`, bound to `prayerMode` and `setPrayerMode`, the same field the reader's header switch writes, with `settings.prayerModeHint` under them) and the siddur reading controls (auto-scroll switch and [ScrollSpeedStepper](../../components/ScrollSpeedStepper.tsx), 15 levels, writing the same `siddurAutoScroll` and `siddurScrollSpeed` the reader's header controls write). The mitzvot card holds one [ListRow](../../components/ListRow.tsx) (`settings.mitzvotRow` with the count of enabled mitzvot, opens `/mitzvot`) and the `hilulotEnabled` switch (`hilulot.notifications`, the same flag the hilulot screen writes). Device holds four sub-headings, each a [SectionLabel](../../components/SectionLabel.tsx) in `goldText` over a hairline, so they read apart from the plain field labels: `settings.zmanim` (location name and status, "locate me", the city chips and the Kriat Shema opinion chips), `settings.notifications` (the toggle, the OS status, the open-OS-settings button, the Android battery-optimisation row and the platform hint; the permission status refreshes through `useFocusEffect` whenever the tab gains focus, so there is no refresh button), `settings.display` (the [ThemeSwatchRow](../../components/ThemeSwatchRow.tsx) and the language chips) and `settings.app` (the `settings.logout` button and the version line).
- Settings subscribes through one `useShallow` slice of the user store, never `useUserStore()` whole, which re-rendered every section on each permission sync; the enabled-mitzvot count is its own selector on `useMitzvotStore` that returns a number. The reset button is worded as resetting the app: there is no account to log out of, and its confirmation is a destructive [ConfirmDialog](../../components/ConfirmDialog.tsx) that stays busy while `AppResetService.reset()` runs. The taharah block renders only while `taharahOffered()` holds and shows the tracking switch, the disclaimer and, while tracking is on, a button to `/taharah/settings`. The gender and status chips, the tracking switch and the nusach chips call [taharahOptIn.ts](../../stores/taharahOptIn.ts), the same functions the onboarding steps use. Field labels are [SectionLabel](../../components/SectionLabel.tsx)s, and a screen-local `SwitchRow` and `PrimaryButton` serve the repeated rows.
- The theme picker is [ThemeSwatchRow](../../components/ThemeSwatchRow.tsx): unlabelled colour circles for the six palettes, each writing `useUserStore.setTheme`. There is no system / light / dark mode switch.
- Home's blocks run in one order: the [NavBar](../../components/NavBar.tsx), the last-error [Banner](../../components/Banner.tsx) when `useLastError()` holds a `schedule` or `reset` failure (`home.lastError`, `warning` tone, always above the priority banner; a tap clears the error and, for `schedule`, re-runs `NotificationScheduler.rebuild()`), at most one priority [Banner](../../components/Banner.tsx), `home.relevantNow` with the live cards or the empty state, one slim next-up [ListRow](../../components/ListRow.tsx) in a bordered card (`home.nextUp` with `home.nextAt`, the name and the window's opening time; a tap opens the details and a long press opens the same quick sheet as on a card), `home.missed` and `home.completed` (each section, heading included, is absent while its list is empty) and the taharah card last. The priority banner is the first that holds of missing zmanim (a tap opens Settings, where the city and location are chosen), denied notifications, an open check-in, a missing location, a GPS timeout, a ready update and the profile prompt; the rest wait until it clears. The taharah card shows while `taharahEnabled` holds and no quiet block does: the cycle stage, then the first unfinished task of today, else the stage's hint. While `useTaharahStore.lockEnabled` holds it shows only the `taharah.home.title` heading and the `taharah.home.open` caption, because the session is always locked while home is showing. It opens `/taharah`. Its cycle, task and perisha data come from [../../utils/AGENTS.md](../../utils/AGENTS.md); home adds only the wording.
- Home offers a gold banner (`home.completeProfile`) into settings while onboarded and either the gender or the marital status is unanswered. It asks for the profile answers and never names the taharah topic, which a single user must not see. Pressing it writes the MMKV key `profile:intro-dismissed`, read once into state at mount, so it appears once; the key names the question set, so a new question gets a new key and every install is asked once more.
- Home's current and missed cards offer the open-text button only when `hasSiddurText()` holds for today, and open `/siddur/[id]` with today's `dateKey`, the key the cards and notifications already use.
- The mitzvot library is [../mitzvot.tsx](../mitzvot.tsx), a stack route opened from the settings row and the onboarding ready step; the siddur tab is [siddur.tsx](siddur.tsx), with the today group first.
- The siddur tab, [siddur.tsx](siddur.tsx), opens with a [NavBar](../../components/NavBar.tsx) (`siddur.catalog.title`, `siddur.catalog.subtitle` and the nusach) and lists `siddur.group.today` first: every `MITZVOT` entry that has a siddur text, each at its `currentOrNextWindow()`, as a [ListRow](../../components/ListRow.tsx) captioned `siddur.today.open` while that window is open, `siddur.today.later` with the opening time when `hasSiddurText()` holds for a later window (`siddur.today.laterOn` with `common.tomorrow` or the weekday name before the time when that window opens on another day), and `siddur.catalog.notToday` (dimmed, not pressable) when it does not. Rows sort by window start with a mitzvah that has no window last, a tap opens `/siddur/[id]` with the window's `dateKey` as `date`, and the `useNow` tick recomputes them. The standalone groups follow: every `STANDALONE_TEXTS` entry under its `SIDDUR_GROUPS` heading (a group with no text is not drawn), named in the UI language. A text whose `available` condition fails for its `standaloneTextDay()` is dimmed, cannot be opened and shows its `availableLabel` (else `siddur.catalog.notToday`); the shared `useNow` tick re-checks it when a day turns. A `/siddur` deep link reaches the tab through the redirect in [../siddur/index.tsx](../siddur/index.tsx).

## Work Guidance

- Keep tab screens scannable and mobile-first; avoid turning operational app screens into marketing layouts.
- Preserve Hebrew-first and RTL ergonomics.
- If a tab consumes notification, location, completion, or settings state, use the owning store/service APIs instead of reaching into persistence directly.
