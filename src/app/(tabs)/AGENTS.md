# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own the main bottom-tab experience: more, siddur, home, history, settings, and tab layout.

## Ownership

- [_layout.tsx](_layout.tsx) owns tab registration and tab bar behavior.
- [more.tsx](more.tsx), [history.tsx](history.tsx), [home.tsx](home.tsx), [siddur.tsx](siddur.tsx), and [settings.tsx](settings.tsx) own their respective tab screens. The schedule is the stack route [../schedule.tsx](../schedule.tsx), opened from More and owned by [../AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
- [index.tsx](index.tsx) redirects hidden tab index traffic.

## Local Contracts

- Tab labels and normal UI copy must stay in [../../i18n/AGENTS.md](../../i18n/AGENTS.md).

- More, [more.tsx](more.tsx), is a hub of rows that open stack screens (the user's call, 2026-10-11): a [NavBar](../../components/NavBar.tsx) titled `more.title`, then one `divided` [Card](../../components/Card.tsx) holding one [ListRow](../../components/ListRow.tsx) with `chevron` per destination. Its rows are the schedule (the `calendar` icon, `schedule.title` captioned `more.scheduleCaption`, a tap pushes `/schedule`) Chok LeYisrael, always shown: the `chok` icon (`accent` once the add-on is installed, `muted` before), `chok.title`, and, installed, the caption of this moment's portion (`chokPortionAt()`: `chok.parashaOf` with the week's names and the slot's `chok.slot.*`, or `chok.nothingToday`) on the `useNow` tick and a tap that opens `/siddur/chok_leyisrael`; not installed, `chokAddonCaption()` (the size to download, the progress, or the failure) and a tap that starts the download in place through `useChokDownloadFlow()` (manifest fetch, then the size confirmation dialog the screen renders once; the row is `disabled` while the manifest loads), or opens `/mitzvah/chok_leyisrael`, where the progress and cancel live, while a download runs and on web. Focusing the tab runs `checkChokUpdate()`, at most one version check a day; last, Tehillim (the user's request, 2026-10-12): the `tehillim` icon, the registry name of `STANDALONE_TEXTS.tehillim` in the UI language, captioned `more.tehillimCaption`, and a tap opens `/siddur/tehillim`, the same reader and text as the siddur tab's row; and last, the halacha hotline (the user's request, 2026-10-14): the `phone` icon, `more.halacha.title`, captioned `more.halacha.caption.<ruling>`, and a tap opens a [BottomSheet](../../components/BottomSheet.tsx) of the lines of `halachaRulingFor(nusach)` ([halachaLines.ts](../../data/halachaLines.ts)), each under a [SectionLabel](../../components/SectionLabel.tsx) with its hours, a `SheetAction` that dials it (`more.halacha.call`) and, when the line has one, one that opens its WhatsApp chat (`more.halacha.whatsapp`), both through `Linking.openURL()`. ## Work Guidance

- Keep tab screens scannable and mobile-first; avoid turning operational app screens into marketing layouts.
- Preserve Hebrew-first and RTL ergonomics.
- If a tab consumes notification, location, completion, or settings state, use the owning store/service APIs instead of reaching into persistence directly.
