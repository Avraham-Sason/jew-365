# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own static app registries and conversion logic for mitzvot, cities, nuschaot, and custom mitzvah adaptation.

## Ownership

- [mitzvot.ts](mitzvot.ts) owns the central static mitzvah registry.
- [cities.ts](cities.ts) owns supported city/location presets.
- [nuschaot.ts](nuschaot.ts) owns nusach metadata.
- [customMitzvotAdapter.ts](customMitzvotAdapter.ts) adapts persisted custom mitzvot into the shared mitzvah shape.
- [siddur.ts](siddur.ts) owns the siddur text registries: `SIDDUR_TEXTS` (which mitzvot have a text, whether it is an evening text, and on which days it is unavailable) with `mitzvahTextId()` and `hasSiddurText()`, and `STANDALONE_TEXTS` (texts tied to no mitzvah: name in both languages, `SiddurGroup`, `evening` for a text said at night, an optional `available` condition and the `availableLabel` the catalog shows when it fails) with `standaloneTextId()`, `standaloneTextsIn(group)`, `SIDDUR_GROUPS`, `hasStandaloneText(id, nusach, features)` and `standaloneTextDay(id, instant, location)`, the Hebrew day a text opened with no date resolves for: `HebcalService.hebrewNightAt()` for an evening text, else `hebrewDayAt()`. `SIDDUR_TEXT_IDS` lists both, and every id ships for all four nuschaot. It also owns `siddurPlace()` (the Israel and Jerusalem facts the day flags need, from the user's settings and location) and the custom-mitzvah text adapter.
- The weekday services (shacharit, mincha, maariv) are unavailable on Shabbat, Yom Tov and Yom Kippur; maariv is judged by the Hebrew day its evening opens.
- [taharahPresets.ts](taharahPresets.ts) owns `TAHARAH_PRESETS`, the `TaharahRules` of each preset (`ashkenaz`, `chassidic`, `chabad`, `sephardi_ovadia`, `sephardi_eliyahu`), `TAHARAH_PRESET_IDS`, `presetForNusach()` (only a suggested starting point from the prayer nusach: Sefard suggests `chassidic`, Edot HaMizrach `sephardi_ovadia`), `rulesFor()` (a copy a caller may edit without touching the preset) and `TAHARAH_RULES_VERSION`. The engine that reads the rules is [../utils/taharah/](../utils/taharah/).
- [hilulot.ts](hilulot.ts) owns `HILULOT`, the core list of tzaddikim (one list for every nusach, the user's choice on 2026-10-08) with each Hebrew date of death, and `hilulaDayIn()`, `hilulotOn()`, `upcomingHilulot()` and `hilulaDateLabel()`. A hilula falls by hebcal's `getYahrzeit()`, except that a plain Adar date moves to Adar II in Israel in a leap year (Adar I abroad), as the Mishna Berura records the custom for 7 Adar. `getYahrzeit()` rewrites the HDate it is given, so each call gets a fresh one.
- [siddurAssets.generated.ts](siddurAssets.generated.ts) is generated by the build in [../../scripts/siddur/AGENTS.md](../../scripts/siddur/AGENTS.md). Never edit it by hand.
- Tests live in [__tests__/](__tests__/).

## Local Contracts

- Static mitzvah changes must preserve the `Mitzvah` contract from [../types/mitzvah.ts](../types/mitzvah.ts).
- Mitzvah windows must avoid UTC date shortcuts and stay compatible with [../services/ZmanimService.ts](../services/ZmanimService.ts) and [../services/HebcalService.ts](../services/HebcalService.ts).
- In a static `computeWindow`, ask every calendar question (weekday, next day, `isYomTov`, Omer count) about `dayOf(ctx.zmanim, location)`: the location's day those zmanim belong to, at its midday. Never use `ctx.date`. Its device-local getters read the wrong day once the zones differ, and its clock time depends on the caller (midnight, now, or a rebuild's evening). An evening `ctx.date` already sits in the next Hebrew day, which once erased erev-Yom-Tov candle lighting.
- Candle lighting opens only on the erev of a holy block (the day is not holy and the next is), and havdalah only on the block's last day, plus a Sunday on which the Tisha B'Av fast falls (9 Av, or 10 Av deferred from Shabbat), because motzaei Shabbat had only the flame. Havdalah's notification lines never mention Shabbat: it also closes Yom Tov, Yom Kippur and that fast. Both ask `HebcalService.isHolyDay()` about `dayOf()` days. Every window that opens at night (maariv, the Omer, havdalah) starts at `HebcalService.nightfall()`: tzeit, or the block's exit (ten minutes after tzeit) on a block's last day, so no reminder lands strictly inside the quiet block; the Sunday Tisha B'Av havdalah keeps tzeit, since the fast, not Shabbat, ends then.
- A reminder must fall inside its own window, so the candle-lighting reminder fires at lighting time only; the scheduler's pre-block notice carries the lead time.
- `defaultReminders` hold the notification copy and this registry is its only source: `label` is the line the mitzvah screen shows and the fallback body, `bodyVariants` are the three bodies the scheduler rotates by trigger day (`pickBodyForReminder()`); the first variant equals the reminder's `label`, pinned by [__tests__/mitzvot.test.ts](__tests__/mitzvot.test.ts). The first reminder of every daily mitzvah and of the Omer carries three variants, plain and short, with the mitzvah named in the first. A follow-up label that states a duration (`נותרה שעה`, `45 דק'`) must match its `offsetMin`, which [__tests__/mitzvotExtras.test.ts](__tests__/mitzvotExtras.test.ts) checks. Copy is Hebrew only; the scheduler builds the English body from the mitzvah's English name. Changing the copy needs no `SCHEDULE_FORMAT` raise: the next daily rebuild schedules the new text.
- Never build a night-time window end with `Date.setDate(+1)` on `zmanim.chatzot` — that is midday chatzot and yields the next day's noon. Use `zmanim.chatzotLayla` (solar midnight, already the next civil date) or the next day's `alotHaShachar`.
- `omerDayFor()` returns the count due on the night that OPENS at tzeit of the given day, which belongs to the next Hebrew day: night 1 on the evening of 15 Nisan, night 49 on the evening of 4 Sivan. Do not re-anchor it to the daytime Hebrew date.
- When a screen or service must include custom mitzvot, use `getAllMitzvot()` or `findAnyMitzvah()`.
- `hasSiddurText()` is the single answer to "does this reminder offer a text": the scheduler category (withheld only on a holy block's opening edge, where the reader is unreachable), the mitzvah screen button and the reader all use it. A custom mitzvah offers its own non-link content blocks.
- Changing any preset value in [taharahPresets.ts](taharahPresets.ts) raises `TAHARAH_RULES_VERSION` and the values pinned in [__tests__/taharahPresets.test.ts](__tests__/taharahPresets.test.ts), so a stored rule set can tell that it predates the change. The values are initial, pending a rav's review.
- Every `HILULOT` date was checked against at least two sources on 2026-10-08. Open for review by the user or a rav: the leap-year Adar placement above; King David and the Baal Shem Tov on 6 Sivan (some say 7); Shmuel HaNavi on 28 Iyar (some say 29); Rashbi on Lag BaOmer, whose source the Chida questioned; Rabbi Meir Baal HaNes on Pesach Sheni, a custom with no early source; Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi on 15 Kislev and the Ramban on 11 Nisan, dates found only in late sources; and the Shelah on 11 Nisan, the tombstone's date, which some historians doubt.
- New user-facing labels or text must be reflected in [../i18n/AGENTS.md](../i18n/AGENTS.md) when they are normal UI copy.
