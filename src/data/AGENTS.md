# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own static app registries and conversion logic for mitzvot, cities, nuschaot, and custom mitzvah adaptation.

## Ownership

- [mitzvot.ts](mitzvot.ts) owns the central static mitzvah registry. `CHOK_LEYISRAEL` (`chok_leyisrael`, the reader's `CHOK_TEXT_ID`) joins `MITZVOT` only while `hasChokContent()` holds: that one predicate keeps a hidden, disabled, windowless mitzvah off every surface that lists mitzvot. It stays exported for its suite.
- [cities.ts](cities.ts) owns supported city/location presets. Every `CITIES` entry is a `City` (a `Location` with a required `cityId`): a stable, unique, lowercase ASCII slug written out once from the English name and never derived again, so renaming a city keeps the id a saved location holds; [__tests__/cities.test.ts](__tests__/cities.test.ts) pins every id. `findCityById()` and `findCityByName()` look an entry up. `cityOf(location)` finds the entry a saved or GPS location stands for: by `cityId` when it has one (an id no city holds finds none), else by exact Hebrew `name`, then `nameEn`, for a snapshot saved before ids existed. `refreshSavedLocation(saved, source)` is the one hydration rule, called by [../stores/useUserStore.ts](../stores/useUserStore.ts): a manual pick becomes the current entry, a GPS fix keeps its own `lat` / `lng` and takes every other field (`name`, `nameEn`, `tz`, `inIsrael`, `elevation`, `candleLightingMinutes`, `cityId`) from the entry, and a location that matches no city (a far fix, "current location") comes back unchanged. `resolvedLocation()` in [../services/LocationService.ts](../services/LocationService.ts) stamps the same `cityId` and `candleLightingMinutes` on a GPS fix within 25 km of a city. A corrected value in `CITIES` therefore reaches every install on its next launch.
- [nuschaot.ts](nuschaot.ts) owns nusach metadata.
- [customMitzvotAdapter.ts](customMitzvotAdapter.ts) adapts persisted custom mitzvot into the shared mitzvah shape. Its category is derived from the hours by `categoryForWindow()` in [../utils/customMitzvah.ts](../utils/customMitzvah.ts) (a stale `category` key on a stored mitzvah is ignored), and its `computeWindow` returns `null` on a weekday missing from `activeDays`, judged on the location's calendar day (the day the window starts), so every surface skips that day like any other `null` window. A window ends the next calendar day, at the end clock in the location's zone, when the end is at or before the start (`22:00`–`01:00`; equal times are 24 hours), so a DST change inside it moves the real length, not the wall-clock end; the spring-forward collapse guard keeps `windowMinutes()` of the configured hours. It drops any `bodyVariants` a reminder carries: rotating bodies belong to the registry mitzvot only (the user's call, 2026-10-11), so a custom reminder always notifies with its `label`, and a custom mitzvah saved before that date loses the variants it stored.
- [siddur.ts](siddur.ts) owns the siddur text registries: `SIDDUR_TEXTS` (which mitzvot have a text, whether it is an evening text, and on which days it is unavailable) with `mitzvahTextId()` and `hasSiddurText()`, and `STANDALONE_TEXTS` (texts tied to no mitzvah: name in both languages, `SiddurGroup`, `evening` for a text said at night, an optional `available` condition and the `availableLabel` the catalog shows when it fails) with `standaloneTextId()`, `standaloneTextsIn(group)`, `SIDDUR_GROUPS` (meals, general, occasions: the order of the catalog after its today and prayers cards, which are mitzvah texts; a group lists its texts in the key order of `STANDALONE_TEXTS`, so the general group reads Tikkun HaKlali, Tehillim (right after it, the user's call on 2026-10-12), the bedtime Shema, the traveler's prayer, Asher Yatzar, the food blessings, the sights, the mezuzah and Kiddush Levana, the user's call on 2026-10-12), `hasStandaloneText(id, nusach, features)` and `standaloneTextDay(id, instant, location)`, the Hebrew day a text opened with no date resolves for: `HebcalService.hebrewNightAt()` for an evening text, else `hebrewDayAt()`. `SIDDUR_TEXT_IDS` lists both, `isSiddurTextId()` tells whether a stored id still names one (a custom mitzvah's siddur block may hold a retired id such as `tzitzit`), and every id ships for all four nuschaot (a text the same in all of them, such as Tikkun HaKlali and Tehillim, as one shared asset the generated map gives each). It also owns `siddurPlace()` (the Israel and Jerusalem facts the day flags need, from the user's settings and location) and the custom-mitzvah text adapter.
- The weekday services (shacharit, mincha, maariv) are unavailable on Shabbat, Yom Tov and Yom Kippur; maariv is judged by the Hebrew day its evening opens.
- [taharahPresets.ts](taharahPresets.ts) owns `TAHARAH_PRESETS`, the `TaharahRules` of each preset (`ashkenaz`, `chassidic`, `chabad`, `sephardi_ovadia`, `sephardi_eliyahu`), `TAHARAH_PRESET_IDS`, `presetForNusach()` (only a suggested starting point from the prayer nusach: Sefard suggests `chassidic`, Edot HaMizrach `sephardi_ovadia`), `rulesFor()` (a copy a caller may edit without touching the preset) and `TAHARAH_RULES_VERSION`. The engine that reads the rules is [../utils/taharah/](../utils/taharah/).
- [hilulot.ts](hilulot.ts) owns `HILULOT`, the core list of tzaddikim (one list for every nusach, the user's choice on 2026-10-08) with each Hebrew date of death, and `hilulaDayIn()`, `hilulotOn()`, `upcomingHilulot()` and `hilulaDateLabel()`. A hilula falls by hebcal's `getYahrzeit()`, except that a plain Adar date moves to Adar II in Israel in a leap year (Adar I abroad), as the Mishna Berura records the custom for 7 Adar. `getYahrzeit()` rewrites the HDate it is given, so each call gets a fresh one.
- [roshChodesh.ts](roshChodesh.ts) owns the Rosh Chodesh day arithmetic, pure over `HDate`: `isRoshChodeshDay()` (the 30th of a full month and the 1st of every month but Tishrei, which hebcal agrees with on every day of ten checked years), `startsRoshChodesh()` (the first day of a one- or two-day Rosh Chodesh), `roshChodeshMonth()` (the 1st of the new month both days announce), `roshChodeshLabel()` (the month name without nikud in Hebrew, the two Adars of a leap year as `אדר א׳` / `אדר ב׳`, and hebcal's English name) and `upcomingRoshChodesh()` (the next occurrences whose last day is today or later, with `days` 1 or 2).
- [siddurAssets.generated.ts](siddurAssets.generated.ts) is generated by the build in [../../scripts/siddur/AGENTS.md](../../scripts/siddur/AGENTS.md): `SIDDUR_ASSETS` by nusach and text id, and `SHARED_SIDDUR_TEXT_IDS`, the texts whose four entries name one shared file. Never edit it by hand.
- [chok.ts](chok.ts) owns the Chok LeYisrael registry glue: `CHOK_TEXT_ID` (`chok_leyisrael`, the reader id), `chokAsset(parasha)` (a parasha's asset module by its hebcal name) and `hasChokContent()` (the generated map is not empty). Every Chok LeYisrael surface hides itself while it is false.
- [chokAssets.generated.ts](chokAssets.generated.ts) is generated by the build in [../../scripts/chok/AGENTS.md](../../scripts/chok/AGENTS.md). Until the source's owner gives written permission the committed version is the empty map (`pnpm chok:build --empty`); never commit it with entries before then, and never edit it by hand.
- Tests live in [__tests__/](__tests__/).

## Local Contracts

- Static mitzvah changes must preserve the `Mitzvah` contract from [../types/mitzvah.ts](../types/mitzvah.ts).
- Mitzvah windows must avoid UTC date shortcuts and stay compatible with [../services/ZmanimService.ts](../services/ZmanimService.ts) and [../services/HebcalService.ts](../services/HebcalService.ts).
- In a static `computeWindow`, ask every calendar question (weekday, next day, `isYomTov`, Omer count) about `dayOf(ctx.zmanim, location)`: the location's day those zmanim belong to, at its midday. Never use `ctx.date`. Its device-local getters read the wrong day once the zones differ, and its clock time depends on the caller (midnight, now, or a rebuild's evening). An evening `ctx.date` already sits in the next Hebrew day, which once erased erev-Yom-Tov candle lighting.
- Candle lighting opens only on the erev of a holy block (the day is not holy and the next is), and havdalah only on the block's last day, plus a Sunday on which the Tisha B'Av fast falls (9 Av, or 10 Av deferred from Shabbat), because motzaei Shabbat had only the flame. Havdalah's notification lines never mention Shabbat: it also closes Yom Tov, Yom Kippur and that fast. Both ask `HebcalService.isHolyDay()` about `dayOf()` days. Every window that opens at night (maariv, the Omer, havdalah) starts at `HebcalService.nightfall()`: tzeit, or the block's exit (ten minutes after tzeit) on a block's last day, so no reminder lands strictly inside the quiet block; the Sunday Tisha B'Av havdalah keeps tzeit, since the fast, not Shabbat, ends then.
- A reminder must fall inside its own window, so the candle-lighting reminder fires at lighting time only; the scheduler's pre-block notice carries the lead time.
- The Chok LeYisrael window is the reader's learning day: from the day's `alotHaShachar` to `nextAlot()` (the next day's alot, shared with the Omer), or to the start of the holy block that opens that evening (`HebcalService.holyBlockOn()` of the next day, so candle lighting is never re-derived). It is `null` while `!hasChokContent()` and when `chokPortionOn()` of the `dayOf()` day is `null` (Shabbat, Yom Tov, the Friday of a Vezot Haberakhah week). Its evening reminder is a `clock` reminder at 20:30 on the location's clock (the user's call, 2026-10-12; `start` + 945 minutes swung between 17:03 and 22:00 in London); the morning one stays `start` + 240. It is dropped, like any trigger outside its window, when the window ends first at candle lighting: on every Friday and erev of the year in Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Los Angeles and New York. On a summer Friday in London, where candle lighting is after 20:30, it fires (21 May to 30 July in 2027).
- `defaultReminders` hold the notification copy and this registry is its only source: `label` is the line the mitzvah screen shows and the fallback body, `bodyVariants` are the three bodies the scheduler rotates by trigger day (`pickBodyForReminder()`); the first variant equals the reminder's `label`, pinned by [__tests__/mitzvot.test.ts](__tests__/mitzvot.test.ts). The first reminder of every daily mitzvah and of the Omer carries three variants, plain and short, with the mitzvah named in the first. A follow-up label that states a duration (`נותרה שעה`, `45 דק'`) must match its `offsetMin`, which [__tests__/mitzvotExtras.test.ts](__tests__/mitzvotExtras.test.ts) checks. Copy is Hebrew only; the scheduler builds the English body from the mitzvah's English name. A `clock` reminder's `offsetMin` is an integer from 0 to 1439, which [__tests__/mitzvotExtras.test.ts](__tests__/mitzvotExtras.test.ts) checks too. Changing the copy or a reminder's time needs no `SCHEDULE_FORMAT` raise: the next daily rebuild schedules the new one.
- Never build a night-time window end with `Date.setDate(+1)` on `zmanim.chatzot` — that is midday chatzot and yields the next day's noon. Use `zmanim.chatzotLayla` (solar midnight, already the next civil date) or the next day's `alotHaShachar`.
- `omerDayFor()` returns the count due on the night that OPENS at tzeit of the given day, which belongs to the next Hebrew day: night 1 on the evening of 15 Nisan, night 49 on the evening of 4 Sivan. Do not re-anchor it to the daytime Hebrew date.
- When a screen or service must include custom mitzvot, use `getAllMitzvot()` or `findAnyMitzvah()`.
- `hasSiddurText()` is the single answer to "does this reminder offer a text": the scheduler category (withheld only on a holy block's opening edge, where the reader is unreachable), the mitzvah screen button and the reader all use it. A custom mitzvah offers its own text and blessing blocks; a link or siddur block never makes a text. For `chok_leyisrael` it is true when the window's date is not Shabbat, Yom Tov or Yom Kippur by `dayFeatures()` and `chokDayHasText()` finds a portion on it whose every parasha is in the generated map; the reminder's open text and body tap then reach `/siddur/chok_leyisrael?date=<dateKey>`.
- Changing any preset value in [taharahPresets.ts](taharahPresets.ts) raises `TAHARAH_RULES_VERSION` and the values pinned in [__tests__/taharahPresets.test.ts](__tests__/taharahPresets.test.ts), so a stored rule set can tell that it predates the change. The values are initial, pending a rav's review.
- Every `HILULOT` date was checked against at least two sources on 2026-10-08. Open for review by the user or a rav: the leap-year Adar placement above; King David and the Baal Shem Tov on 6 Sivan (some say 7); Shmuel HaNavi on 28 Iyar (some say 29); Rashbi on Lag BaOmer, whose source the Chida questioned; Rabbi Meir Baal HaNes on Pesach Sheni, a custom with no early source; Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi on 15 Kislev and the Ramban on 11 Nisan, dates found only in late sources; and the Shelah on 11 Nisan, the tombstone's date, which some historians doubt.
- New user-facing labels or text must be reflected in [../i18n/AGENTS.md](../i18n/AGENTS.md) when they are normal UI copy.
